Privacy Policy

Mesquite Ridge Agent (Demo) · Effective date: October 14, 2026

Mesquite Ridge Insurance Agency is a fictional business. This WhatsApp assistant is a portfolio demonstration project built by Lydiah Nganga. It does not sell insurance, bind coverage, or provide quotes.

What information is collected

When you send a message to the demo WhatsApp number, the system receives your WhatsApp phone number, your display name, and the content of your text or voice messages. If you ask to be contacted, you may also choose to share your name, email, ZIP code, and type of insurance you are interested in.

How it is used

Your messages are used only to generate replies in the demo conversation and to keep short-term conversation history so the assistant can follow the discussion. Voice messages are transcribed to text for the same purpose. If you request a callback, the details you shared are sent by email to the project owner for demonstration purposes only.

Service providers

The demo relies on third-party services to work: Meta (WhatsApp Business Platform), OpenAI (language model, embeddings and voice transcription), Google (Gmail for callback emails), and Supabase (database). These providers process data under their own terms and privacy policies.

Retention and deletion

Conversation history is stored in a database for the duration of the demo and can be deleted at any time. To request deletion of your data, email the contact address below with the phone number you used, and it will be removed.

Sensitive information

Please do not share Social Security numbers, driver's license numbers, payment card details, or bank information with this assistant. It is not designed to receive them.

No sale of data

Your information is not sold or shared for advertising.
