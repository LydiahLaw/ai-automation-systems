# Mali Travels — Knowledge Base Q&A

## BOOKINGS & RESERVATIONS

**Q: How do I book a trip with Mali Travels?**
A: You can book directly through this chat, by calling us at +254 700 123 456, or by visiting our website. We recommend booking at least 2 weeks in advance for safari packages, and at least 1 week for coastal getaways, to guarantee availability.

**Q: What's your cancellation policy?**
A: Cancellations made 14+ days before departure receive a full refund minus a 10% processing fee. Cancellations within 7-13 days receive a 50% refund. Cancellations within 6 days of departure are non-refundable, though we can help you reschedule where possible.

**Q: Can I modify my booking after confirming?**
A: Yes. Contact us at least 5 days before departure to change dates, group size, or package details, subject to availability. Changes made within 5 days of departure may incur a rebooking fee.

**Q: What's the minimum and maximum group size?**
A: We accommodate solo travelers up to groups of 12. Groups larger than 8 should contact us directly to discuss vehicle and accommodation logistics.

**Q: Do I need a deposit to confirm my booking?**
A: Yes, a 30% deposit is required to confirm any booking, with the balance due 7 days before departure. Deposits can be paid via M-Pesa, bank transfer, or major credit cards.

## PACKAGES & PRICING

**Q: What packages do you offer?**
A: Our most popular packages are:
- **3-Day Maasai Mara Safari** — game drives, cultural village visit, all meals and park fees included. From KES 45,000 per person.
- **5-Day Coastal Escape (Diani & Watamu)** — beachfront accommodation, snorkeling excursion, and a dhow sunset cruise. From KES 60,000 per person.
- **7-Day Grand Kenya Safari** — combines Maasai Mara, Lake Nakuru, and Amboseli. From KES 110,000 per person.
- **Day Trips** — Nairobi National Park, Hell's Gate, and Ol Pejeta available as single-day excursions from KES 8,000 per person.

**Q: What's included in the package price?**
A: All packages include transport, park entry fees, accommodation as specified, and meals as listed per itinerary. Flights, travel insurance, personal expenses, and gratuities are not included unless stated.

**Q: Do you offer custom itineraries?**
A: Yes, we can tailor any package to your interests, budget, or timeframe. Custom itineraries typically take 2-3 business days to quote once we understand your requirements.

**Q: Do you have discounts for families or large groups?**
A: Children under 12 receive a 30% discount on most packages. Groups of 6 or more receive a 10% discount on the total package price.

## LOGISTICS & TRAVEL INFO

**Q: What should I pack for a safari?**
A: Neutral-colored clothing (khaki, olive, brown), a warm layer for early morning game drives, sunscreen, insect repellent, a hat, and comfortable closed shoes. We provide a full packing checklist upon booking confirmation.

**Q: Do I need a visa to travel to Kenya?**
A: Most visitors require an eTA (Electronic Travel Authorization) obtained online before arrival. East African Community citizens do not require a visa. We recommend checking current requirements for your nationality at least 4 weeks before travel.

**Q: Where do trips depart from?**
A: All safari packages depart from Nairobi, with pickup available from major hotels or Jomo Kenyatta International Airport. Coastal packages can depart from either Nairobi (by road or flight) or directly from Mombasa.

**Q: What vehicles do you use for safaris?**
A: We use 4x4 safari Land Cruisers with pop-up roofs for optimal game viewing and photography, each with a maximum of 6 passengers to ensure everyone gets a window seat.

## PAYMENT & POLICIES

**Q: What payment methods do you accept?**
A: We accept M-Pesa, bank transfer, Visa, and Mastercard. International clients can also pay via bank wire transfer.

**Q: Is travel insurance required?**
A: We strongly recommend travel insurance covering medical evacuation, but it is not mandatory to book with us. We can recommend providers upon request.

**Q: Do you offer a loyalty or referral program?**
A: Yes — returning clients receive a 5% discount on their next booking, and referrals who book a package earn both the referrer and the new client a 5% discount.
